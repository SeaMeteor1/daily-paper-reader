## 摘要
具身机器人越来越多地依赖大语言模型（LLM）来理解用户意图，并与机器人专用的动作模型协作以完成物理交互。这种多模型流水线在心智、模型协作与运动之间形成了关键的语义边界：在大模型所理解的内容、小型动作模型如何将该理解转化为可执行命令，以及机器人最终执行的物理动作之间，语义一致性可能会断裂。作为安全后果，即使用户输入不包含显式动作命令，商用机器人也可能执行非预期的物理动作。为度量该问题，我们开发了 TARS，一个黑盒框架，用于探索命令-推理-动作流水线，并自动发现和验证非预期物理行为。我们在四款商用机器人上于 44 种实验设置中评估 TARS，共执行 133,400 次运行。TARS 揭示了广泛的跨模型动作失配，并实现了最高 94% 的验证动作覆盖率。

## Abstract
Embodied robots increasingly rely on large language models (LLMs) to interpret user intent and coordinate with robot-specific action models for physical interaction. This multi-model pipeline creates a critical semantic boundary across mind, model collaboration, and motion: semantic consistency may break between what the large model understands, how the small action model translates that understanding into executable commands, and what physical actions the robot ultimately performs. As a security consequence, commercial robots may execute unintended physical actions even when user inputs contain no explicit action commands. To measure this problem, we develop TARS, a black-box framework that explores the command-reasoning-action pipeline and automatically discovers and validates unintended physical behaviors. We evaluate TARS on four commercial robots across 44 experimental settings, conducting 133,400 executions. TARS uncovers widespread cross-model action misalignment and achieves up to 94% verified action coverage.
