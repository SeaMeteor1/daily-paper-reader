## 摘要
协作移动操作要求机器人协调运动，同时确保全身避碰并保持任务相关行为。我们提出 CALDS（Cooperative Adaptation of Learned Dynamical Systems，学习型动力系统的协作自适应），这是一个使移动操作机器人能够调整已学习的单智能体行为以进行协调执行的框架。CALDS 基于从演示中学习的个体动力系统（DS），通过两个关键组件实现协作自适应：协作策略选择，用于识别已学习策略的兼容组合；以及时序自适应，用于在保持原始行为的空间结构和收敛性质的同时确保无碰撞协作。我们在三个不同场景中评估 CALDS：辅助穿衣、餐厅清洁与上菜，以及抽屉打开与物品放置。定量结果表明，该方法能够提高任务成功率，并支持安全、协调的同步执行，同时保持对演示行为的保真度。

## Abstract
Cooperative mobile manipulation requires robots to coordinate their motions while ensuring whole-body collision avoidance and preserving task-relevant behaviors. We propose CALDS (Cooperative Adaptation of Learned Dynamical Systems), a framework that enables mobile manipulators to adapt learned single-agent behaviors for coordinated execution. Building on individual dynamical systems (DSs) learned from demonstrations, CALDS enables cooperative adaptation through two key components: Cooperative Policy Selection, which identifies compatible combinations of learned policies, and Temporal Adaptation, which ensures collision-free cooperation while preserving the spatial structure and convergence properties of the original behaviors. We evaluate CALDS across three diverse scenarios: assistive dressing, restaurant cleaning and serving, and drawer opening and object placement. Quantitative results demonstrate its effectiveness in improving task success and enabling safe, coordinated simultaneous execution while maintaining fidelity to demonstrated behaviors.
